Ginkgo Biloba Extract

Ginkgo biloba is a botanical ingredient traditionally used in wellness supplements. It contains plant compounds with antioxidant properties and has been studied for its potential effects on circulation and cognitive health. However, evidence that it improves hearing or treats tinnitus remains insufficient.
🍁

Garlic Extract

Garlic extract contains naturally occurring sulfur compounds and is commonly used in nutritional supplements. It has been studied for potential cardiovascular benefits, including effects on certain blood-pressure and cholesterol measures. It should not be assumed to improve hearing directly.
🍁

Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 is an essential nutrient that supports normal nerve function, red blood cell production, and DNA synthesis. Maintaining adequate levels is important for nervous-system health, particularly in people with a deficiency. Supplementation does not necessarily improve hearing in individuals who already have sufficient B12.
🍁

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 contributes to normal nervous-system function, protein metabolism, and energy metabolism. Adequate intake helps the body perform essential physiological processes. Excessive long-term intake of supplemental vitamin B6 can cause nerve damage, so appropriate dosing is important.
🍁

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in immune function, wound healing, and cellular processes. It also contributes to normal DNA and protein synthesis. Zinc is important for general health, but there is insufficient evidence that zinc supplements reliably restore hearing or prevent hearing loss in people without a deficiency.
🍁

Magnesium

Magnesium supports normal muscle and nerve function, energy production, and electrolyte balance. It plays an important role in numerous processes throughout the body. Although magnesium has been investigated in certain hearing-related contexts, it should not be presented as a proven treatment for hearing loss.
🍁

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is an essential antioxidant nutrient that contributes to normal immune function, collagen formation, and protection of cells from oxidative stress. It supports general health as part of a balanced diet, but its presence in a supplement does not establish that the product improves hearing.
🍁

Folate

Folate, also known as vitamin B9, is necessary for normal cell division, DNA synthesis, and red blood cell formation. Adequate folate intake supports general nutritional health. Its role in maintaining normal body functions should not be confused with proven treatment for auditory disorders.
